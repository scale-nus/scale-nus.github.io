SCALE

Safe Control and Learning Group

Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering
National University of Singapore

How can machines learn from data and still remain provably safe when the world is uncertain? That is the question that inspires our work. At SCALE, we develop the theory and algorithms for safe learning-based control, from Bayesian models of unknown dynamics and event-triggered adaptation to generative models for embodied agents and real-time safety filters.

We bring these ideas to life on robotic systems, with a single vision: autonomy you can trust. Our goal is systems that improve with experience, reason about risk, and keep safety at the core of every decision.

Research Areas

Certifiable Online Learning in Control Systems

Active learning in closed-loop operation to ensure control certificates requires fast algorithms and reliable uncertainty quantification.

Physics-Informed Generative Models

Integrate physics priors into generative models to improve admissibility, reliability, and sample efficiency for embodied deployment.

Safe Probabilistic Learning Control

Develop fast and scalable safety filters for real-time autonomy with formal guarantees despite model uncertainties.

Koopman Operator Methods

Exploit system-theoretic properties to develop model learning techniques tailored for long-horizon prediction accuracy and control design.
